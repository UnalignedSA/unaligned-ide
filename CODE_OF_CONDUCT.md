# Code of Conduct

This applies to everyone who takes part in the Unaligned IDE project, in its repository and in any other space the project uses.

## What is expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas and code, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a language that is not their own.
- Stay on the subject of the project.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change or close a discussion.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or intimidation, in public or in private.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about who someone is.
- Sexual content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam and deliberate disruption.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, commits and other contributions that go against this text, and may block a person from the project's spaces, for a time or for good.

## Reporting a problem

The repository is not public yet. Once it is, reports go through its issue tracker. Do not put private information about anyone in a public report.
